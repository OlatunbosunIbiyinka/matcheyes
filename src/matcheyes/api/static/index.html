              On-Pitch Record events are buttons in time order, home team on the time rail above the pitch and away team on the rail below.               About this replay

              MatchEyes — See beyond the score. A demonstration on fictional football data. The On-Pitch Record holds factual match events; Match Story insights are verified views, shown with their status. Trace the Evidence to inspect the work. The Stat Ledger is context only. All match and insight text comes from the server; this page computes nothing about the match.
